# Your own functions

Functions let you reuse a group of nodes.

## Define

1. Add **Define function** (Functions). Give it a name, parameters (small boxes: type + name, **+** to add) and a return type.
2. Build its body after its **next** pin. Use **Return** to give back a value.

## Call

Add **Call function**, pick your function: its input pins are your parameters. Use it in a hook, a loop, another function.

## Good habits

* Name functions after what they do (`respawnCoins`).
* Select the nodes of a function and press **Ctrl+G** to frame them (see *Groups, comments and the library*).
* Save a function you use in several mods with **Library > Save selection to library**.
